I'm **Zubin Jain**, an ASIC engineer working at the intersection of hardware design and AI-driven tooling. I build high-throughput SystemVerilog RTL for NPU architectures, and I spend a lot of my time figuring out how to make agentic EDA workflows genuinely useful — bringing LLM-assisted refactoring, verification, and toolchain automation into the hardware design loop.

### What I do

- **ASIC / RTL design** — high-throughput SystemVerilog modules for NPU architectures, integrating AXI, APB, and PCIe. UVM verification, coverage closure, and static timing analysis.
- **Agentic EDA & workflows** — pioneering the integration of LLM agents (Claude, Codex) into RTL workflows: legacy code refactoring, early-stage microarchitectural exploration, and CI/regression automation with Jenkins, Python, and Perl.
- **Open-source** — active contributor to [Verilator](https://github.com/verilator/verilator), the industry-leading open-source SystemVerilog simulator.

### Beyond hardware

I'm the **Founder & Editor-in-Chief of *The Sengkang Sci-Fi Journal*** — I built the publication from the ground up, assembled a team of writers, editors, and designers, and run its editorial and publication pipelines. I write science fiction, and I run a couple of book clubs.

### Background

I hold a Bachelor of Electrical Engineering (Honours with Distinction) from the **National University of Singapore**. I'm a Singapore citizen and an Indian OCI cardholder.
